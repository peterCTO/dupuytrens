      Drag to turn the model; scroll or pinch to zoom.

      In a fasciectomy the diseased cord is cut out through the palm. Closing the skin with a Z-plasty swaps two triangular flaps so the scar zig-zags across the line of the finger, lengthening the skin instead of leaving a straight scar that could tighten into a new band.

      In Dupuytren’s disease the palmar fascia thickens into nodules and cords. As a cord contracts it draws the finger into flexion at the knuckle (MCP) and middle joint (PIP), and it stands out under the skin like a bowstring.
